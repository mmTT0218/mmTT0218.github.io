abstract: 'We aim to suppress degradation of 3D image quality caused by rotational misalignment between the parallax barrier and the display in autostereoscopic 3D displays. We propose design guidelines and procedures that flexibly accommodate rotational misalignment between the parallax barrier and the display during assembly, using the parallax barrier’s slant angle as the reference. We evaluated our method under no-misalignment conditions as an initial validation of its effectiveness, comparing previous method by measuring luminance and crosstalk.'
